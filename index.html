Let your viewers play along

CrowdPlay lets Twitch viewers trigger effects in the streamer's game: spawn enemies, hand out items, speed things up or slow them down, and much more.

Twitch Extension in testing

How it works

1

Install CrowdPlay

The streamer runs the CrowdPlay app and the mod for their game on their own PC.

2

Pick the effects

The streamer chooses which effects viewers can use and sets their prices.

3

Viewers redeem

Viewers use Channel Points or Bits. The effect happens live in the game, with their name on it.
